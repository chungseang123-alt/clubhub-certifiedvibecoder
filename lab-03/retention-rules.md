# Club Hub retention rules

| Field | Trigger | Action | Who or what runs it | How to verify |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **studentId** | Account closure; or cancellation / 30 days after the event month ends for registration rows | Delete the student row from data/students.csv on account closure, and delete registration rows in data/registrations.csv on cancellation or expiry. | Account closure handler and clubhub purge (daily) | Close a test account or expire a test booking, run clubhub purge, and check that the student ID is gone from students.csv and registrations.csv. |
| **name** | Account closure, or removal of a registration row | Delete the name from data/students.csv on account closure; event rosters look up names only through active rows in data/registrations.csv. | Account closure handler and roster lookup | Close a test account and open the event roster to confirm the student's name no longer appears. |
| **email** | New email verified, or account closure | Overwrite the old address in data/students.csv once the new one is verified, and delete it when the account closes. | Email verification handler and account closure handler | Change a test account's email, confirm students.csv updates to the new one, then close the account and verify the email is removed. |
| **phone** | SMS opt-out (withdrawSmsConsent()), account closure, or 6 months after graduation | Clear the phone column in data/students.csv, cancel unsent reminders, and request deletion from the SMS gateway after delivery or failure. | Profile handler (withdrawSmsConsent()), account closure handler, and clubhub purge (daily) | Trigger withdrawal on a test account, confirm phone is blank in students.csv, run the reminder sender to see it skip the student, and check the SMS gateway deletion log. |
| **clubMemberships** | Student leaves the club, club closes, or account closes | Delete the membership row from data/memberships.csv. | Membership removal handler and account closure handler | Remove a test membership and verify it disappears from *My clubs*, memberships.csv, and member-only event checks. |
| **attendance** | 1 month after the event ends, or earlier on account closure | Add check-ins to the monthly club count in data/attendance-totals.csv (applying the suppression rule), then delete individual check-in rows from data/registrations.csv. | clubhub purge (daily) and monthly report generator (clubhub report, monthly) | Run clubhub report and clubhub purge on a past event, then check that attendance-totals.csv has the total while registrations.csv has no student check-in rows left. |
| **remindersByEmail** | Student toggles email reminders off (setEmailReminders(false)), or account closes | Set the opt-in flag to false in data/students.csv when turned off, and remove the field when the account closes. | Profile handler (setEmailReminders(false)) and account closure handler | Toggle reminders off in *My profile*, check that students.csv shows false, and confirm the email sender skips that student. |
