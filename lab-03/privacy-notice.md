# Club Hub privacy notice

## Who we are

We run Club Hub and work with student clubs at the Institute of Technology of Cambodia.

## What we collect and why

We use your student ID to identify your account and prevent duplicate sign-ups. We use your name for your profile and event check-in. We use your email to verify your account and send registration confirmations. We use memberships to show your clubs and check access to member-only events. We record check-ins and count attendance for monthly reports. Email and SMS reminders are separate choices. If you choose email reminders, we save your preference. If you choose SMS reminders, we ask for your number and use it to send a reminder 24 hours before an event you signed up for.

## Who sees it

You can see your profile, SMS status and check-ins. Your organiser sees your name, masked ID, check-in and eligibility. Club leaders see their club's memberships. Administrators see account and membership details for support and receive attendance totals. Our email sender uses your address. Our SMS sender shares your number and reminder with the SMS provider for delivery.

## How long

We delete profile details when you close/delete your account. Event rosters expire 30 days after the event month ends or sooner if you cancel or close your account. We delete individual check-ins one month after the event and keep monthly counts. Memberships end when you leave, the club closes or you close your account. We erase your phone number when you withdraw, close your account or reach six months after graduation, whichever comes first.

## Your choices

You can view your details or close your account in My profile, or email us to request deletion
